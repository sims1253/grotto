Warm/cool balance

chroma-weighted score: +0.13

warm (7): diff_added, diff_removed, error, number, string, type, warning
cool (8): bg, comment, constant, fg, function, info, keyword, selection
neutral (0): - ← hue exactly on the 150/330° boundary

Palette score = sum(C_i * cos(h_i - 60deg)) / sum(C_i) over chromatic roles (C >= 0.02). +1 warm (amber), -1 cool (azure), 0 at 150/330 deg. A convention on OKLCH hue, not a perceptual law.
