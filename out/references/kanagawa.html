Warm/cool balance

chroma-weighted score: +0.34

warm (10): constant, diff_added, diff_removed, error, fg, fg_secondary, number, string, success, warning
cool (7): bg_elevated, function, info, keyword, punctuation, selection, type

Palette score = sum(C_i * cos(h_i - 60deg)) / sum(C_i) over chromatic roles (C >= 0.02). +1 warm (amber), -1 cool (azure), 0 at 150/330 deg. A convention on OKLCH hue, not a perceptual law.
